# ApplyFirst Beta Research Priorities

## Next Priority: My Focus Completion

Investigate why only 2 of 13 activated students completed My Focus before changing onboarding.

Research questions:

- Did students understand what My Focus controls and why it is useful?
- Where did activated students stop before saving their class year, role interest, and alert timing?
- Did requiring first and last name, email, and verification make alert setup feel larger than expected?
- Did students review programs or use official-source links without realizing My Focus was separate?
- Were students testing casually, or did they intend to receive ongoing alerts?

Use existing event and participant data first, then conduct short interviews with a small mix of students who completed and did not complete My Focus. Do not change onboarding until the evidence identifies the primary reason for abandonment.
